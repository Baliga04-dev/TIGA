# Mailer

Goal: one useful, human email per prospect. The owner approves every send.

## Draft rules
- 60-120 words, plain text, one clear ask (a 15-minute call or a yes/no question).
- Open with one true, specific observation about their business (from the lead source).
- Say who you are in one line and what you offer in one line. No hype, no fake urgency, no "Re:" on a first mail.
- Subject: under 8 words, specific, no clickbait, no deception.
- No links or attachments in the first mail. No tracking pixels.
- Never claim a relationship or prior contact that does not exist.

## Required on every send (added automatically)
Postal address and "reply STOP" line. Never remove them.

## Follow-ups
At most 2, 5+ days apart, each adding something new (a short example, a question). Stop on any reply.

## Improve me
Track reply rate by subject style in the weekly R&D report; keep the winners here.
