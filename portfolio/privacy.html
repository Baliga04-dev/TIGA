Your details, and how to have them deleted

I'm Adithya Baliga, a freelance web developer in Bengaluru. This page says what I keep about shop owners I talk to, why, for how long, and how to make me delete it.

To have your details deleted, send "delete my number" on WhatsApp to my number, or email ymitigaa@gmail.com. I delete them within 7 days and tell you when it's done. You can also ask what I hold about you.

What I keep

  • Your name, your shop's name and area, and the phone number or email you gave me.
  • What we talked about, and what you agreed to: for example, "yes, WhatsApp me the demo".
  • If you become a client: the work, the quote, invoices and payments.

Where it comes from

From you, when we meet at your shop or when you message me. Before a visit I may note your shop's public listing (OpenStreetMap). I never buy contact lists.

Why I keep it

Only to talk with you about a website, an ordering or booking page, or photo editing for your shop, and, if you hire me, to do the work and send invoices. Nothing else.

How I contact you

Only on the channels you said yes to, by hand, one message at a time, during shop hours. I never send bulk messages or use automatic WhatsApp tools. If you say no, I note it so I never contact you again.

How long I keep it

Until you ask me to delete it. If we never got talking, I delete your details within six months. A "please don't contact me" is kept as a short note, only so I never contact you by mistake.

Who sees it

Only me. Your details are kept on my own computer, and emails stay in my Gmail account. I don't sell or share them. If you agree to let me show your website as my work, I show only what you said yes to.

Last updated 31 October 2026. This notice follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and its rules.
