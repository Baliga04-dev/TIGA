From first call to launch

On the first day we talk for fifteen minutes, free, about your shop and what you want customers to do on the site. You get a one-page written proposal with the price and the dates.

Within a week of the 50% advance and your photos, logo and text, you see a working preview on your own phone, and tell me what to change.

On launch day the site goes live on your own domain, you pay the balance, and every login is handed to you. It’s your site, not mine.

Who builds it

I’m Adithya P Baliga, a developer in Bengaluru. I build websites, booking and ordering apps, and I clean up shop and product photos so your place looks as good online as it does in person.
